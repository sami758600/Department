Logo Mood

Indigo and silver-blue create an academic, thoughtful tone instead of a loud startup style.

Theme Direction

Gold is used only as an accent so the homepage feels elegant and consistent with the mark.

What This Theme Tries To Do

Instead of forcing the website into a generic AI neon look, this sample treats the logo as the visual source of truth and builds a softer academic identity around it.

01

Institution First

The colors feel official and grounded, which suits a department website better than overly flashy futuristic effects.

02

Logo-Led Palette

The page borrows from the logo’s indigo, gray-blue, and muted gold so the whole site feels designed from one system.

03

Calm Premium Feel

Rounded surfaces, layered gradients, and controlled contrast keep the page modern without losing academic seriousness.

Suggested Visual Rules For This Direction

Use dark indigo for headers and emphasis, silver-blue for surfaces and glow, and gold only for highlights, buttons, or key numbers. That keeps the brand feeling elegant rather than noisy.
